Why this practice

Built to answer the three biggest fears in Ghanaian property

Buying land and building in Ghana should not depend on luck. We design every service around the risks clients worry about most.

The fear

"What if the land is disputed or sold twice?"

Multiple sales, unclear boundaries and missing documents turn a dream plot into years of litigation.

We only sell plots whose documents we have checked, and every plot comes with a Land Passport.
The fear

"What if the project runs over budget or cuts corners?"

Weak supervision, unclear costs and poor records mean clients pay for work they cannot inspect.

Independent supervision, milestone inspections and payments released only against documented progress.
The fear

"I live abroad. How do I know my money is being used well?"

Diaspora clients often send funds home with little visibility of what is actually being built.

Regular photo and video reports, a shared project record and a Building Passport at handover.

What a Land Passport covers

Before a plot is listed, we work through each of these steps and share the results with the buyer.

  1. Official searchSearch results from the Lands Commission on the parcel's registered interests.
  2. Site plan and surveyA site plan with coordinates, checked against the land on the ground.
  3. Ownership historyThe chain of ownership and the authority of the person or family selling.
  4. Boundary walkPillars located and neighbours consulted where possible.
  5. Access and servicesRoad access, drainage and utility availability noted honestly.
  6. Title pathwayA clear written plan and timeline for processing the buyer's title.
Whoever you buy from, never pay for land before you have seen the search results and site plan yourself.
How we work

A clear path from first call to handover

Every project moves through the same documented stages. Gold stages are payment gates: money moves only after inspection and sign-off.

  1. ConsultationWe listen to your goals, budget and timeline.
  2. ProposalA written scope, fee and programme.
  3. DesignDrawings, structural design and cost plan.Payment gate
  4. BuildSupervised construction with regular reports.Staged gates
  5. InspectionFinal checks, snag list and certification.Payment gate
  6. HandoverKeys, documents and your Building Passport.

No lump sums, no blind transfers. Construction funds are released stage by stage, each against photos, measurements and an inspection record you can see.
